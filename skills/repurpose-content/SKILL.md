# Repurpose Content

Repurposing badly means posting the same thing in five places. Repurposing well means
finding the five *separate ideas* buried in one piece and giving each the format that
suits it.

## What you need first

The source content, the platforms in play, and the audience. If the source is a
transcript, note that spoken structure is loose — the ideas will need extracting, not
just reformatting.

## Steps

1. **Extract the atomic ideas.** Read the source and list every claim that could stand
   alone. A 1,500-word post usually contains four to seven. If you find one, the source
   is a single idea at length and should become one post, not five.

2. **Rank them by how well they stand without context.** The ones that need the most
   setup are the worst repurposing candidates and the best newsletter sections.

3. **Match idea to format**, not the reverse:
   - A number-led idea → carousel
   - A single counter-intuitive claim → short text post
   - A process → reel or short video, one step per beat
   - A story or a mistake → long-form text post
   - A comparison → table image or side-by-side
   - Everything that did not fit → newsletter section

4. **Write each one natively.** A LinkedIn post is not an Instagram caption with the
   hashtags removed. Different opening, different length, different close.

5. **Vary the hook across the set.** The same hook on five posts reads as automation,
   which is the tell that costs reach.

6. **Space them.** Same-day cross-posting cannibalises; the same idea a week apart on
   two platforms does not.

7. **Flag anything that must not be repurposed** — time-bound claims, anything that
   read as fine in context and reads as overclaiming alone.

## Output format

A table: idea, format, platform, suggested day. Then each post written out in full,
ready to post. Not outlines — the finished text.

## Rules

- Do not invent detail the source does not contain to make a post land better.
- Preserve any hedge in the original. Sources get more confident every time they are
  compressed, and that is how a careful claim becomes a false one in three hops.
- Keep any attribution. If the source cites someone, the repurposed post does too.

## Where this breaks

- **Thin sources.** A 400-word post does not contain a week of content, and pretending
  it does produces five weak posts instead of one good one. Say so.
- **Overlapping audiences.** If the same people follow the user on three platforms,
  aggressive repurposing reads as spam. Vary harder or narrow the platforms.
- **Transcripts carry filler** that reads as insight when written down. Be stricter
  with spoken sources than written ones.
